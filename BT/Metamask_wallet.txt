Practical: Create Your Own Wallet Using MetaMask for Crypto Transactions

2. Practical Procedure
Step 1: Install MetaMask
Go to the official MetaMask website and install the browser extension.

MetaMask official website

Choose your browser and install the extension.
Step 2: Open MetaMask
After installation:
1. Open the MetaMask extension.
2. Select Create a new wallet.
3. Accept the required terms.
4. Create a strong password.
Step 3: Secure Your Wallet
MetaMask will display your Secret Recovery Phrase.

You should:
1. Write it down offline.
2. Keep it somewhere secure.
3. Never share it with anyone.
4. Never enter it into a website claiming to "verify" your wallet.
Then MetaMask will ask you to confirm the phrase.

Once confirmed, your wallet is created.
Step 4: View Your Wallet Address
After creating the wallet, you will see your account.

You can copy the wallet address.

It will look something like:
0xA1B2C3D4E5F6........

This is your public wallet address.

For your practical record, you can write:
Wallet Address:
0x................................

Do not put your Secret Recovery Phrase in your practical record or screenshots.
3. Perform a Crypto Transaction
For a college practical, use a test network/test ETH rather than real cryptocurrency.
Step 1: Select a Test Network
In MetaMask, select an available Ethereum test network such as Sepolia.

The exact interface/network options can change between MetaMask versions.
Step 2: Get Test ETH
Use an appropriate official Ethereum testnet faucet to obtain test ETH.
Open this Sepolia faucet:

Sepolia Faucet

A faucet is simply a website that gives you free test ETH for practice.

The test ETH has no real monetary value.

You generally provide your testnet wallet address and receive test ETH.
Step 3: Create a Second Test Wallet
You can create another account/wallet for demonstration.

For example:
Wallet A → Sender
Wallet B → Receiver

Copy Wallet B's public address.
Step 4: Send Test ETH
From Wallet A:
1. Click Send.
2. Enter Wallet B's public address.
3. Enter a small test amount, for example:
0.001 ETH

4. Review the transaction.
5. Check the estimated gas fee.
6. Click Confirm.
The transaction will be submitted to the blockchain.
Step 5: Verify the Transaction
After confirmation, you can view the transaction details in MetaMask or through a testnet blockchain explorer.

You will typically see:
From:       Wallet A
To:         Wallet B
Amount:     0.001 ETH
Status:     Confirmed
Transaction Hash: 0x........

The transaction hash is a unique identifier for the transaction.
